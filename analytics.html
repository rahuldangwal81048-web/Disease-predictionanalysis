Dataset & Model Analytics

An exploratory look at the symptom–disease dataset in data/diseases.js, followed by a synthetic validation of the Predictor scoring algorithm in js/prediction.js — the kind of EDA and model-evaluation step a data science write-up is expected to show.

Model validation (synthetic test)

Method. The dataset has no real patient records to test against, so validation uses synthetic patients: for every disease, a random subset of its own known symptoms is sampled (simulating a real patient who doesn't report every symptom) and fed back into Predictor.predict() to see whether it recovers the correct condition. Sampling uses a seeded random generator (seed = 42) so results are reproducible on every run. This measures the internal consistency and noise-robustness of the scoring algorithm — it is not a clinical accuracy measurement, since no real diagnosed patient data exists in this educational project.

    !

    About these numbers. All statistics on this page are computed from the project's own small illustrative dataset and a synthetic self-test, not from real patients or clinical trials. They demonstrate how a rule-based scoring system can be explored and validated methodologically — they are not evidence of real-world diagnostic performance.
